# Changelog

One line per version. A mod reaches installed copies through `/plugin update` only when its version moves, so every change to what a mod ships comes with a bump in its `plugin.json` and a line here.

## 2026-10-12

- ant-farm 0.2.9: the pane draws as 0.2.7 did on every surface again (plain buttons with key hints, and long lines on the desktop app may clip at the edge instead of ending with an ellipsis), after the desktop app showed nothing at all with 0.2.8's native buttons and shrinkable boxes; the inspector's next and previous still move from the selection as it stands at the press.
- ant-farm 0.2.8: on the desktop app every button is the app's own kind (the view toggles, next, previous and more did not answer a click as plain buttons there; the terminal keeps its plain buttons with key hints); every box of words may now shrink, so long lines in the header, the cards, the timeline and the inspector end with an ellipsis instead of running past the pane's edge; next and previous move from the selection as it stands when pressed.
- ant-farm 0.2.7: a narrow pane on the desktop app (under 40 columns) wraps its lines instead of cutting them, since the app draws words in a proportional font and clipped them mid-word at the edge; the terminal still cuts a line at its end.
- ant-farm 0.2.6: after a reload of the mod's code mid-session, the pane keeps moving: the clock that walks the ants and the timers starts again when the kept state says something still runs, instead of waiting for the next event.
- ant-farm 0.2.5: a moment after the pane opens it asks to be drawn once more, so a first draw that did not land (seen once on the desktop app, where the pane said nothing had been drawn until it was resized) is drawn after all.
- ant-farm 0.2.4: a subagent held on a permission shows no time left (the estimate climbed while it waited); a finished line keeps its numbers whole wherever they fit and names its model only from 100 columns; no colour of another brand anywhere in the pane (the working state and the thinking word now use the theme's own green and accent keys, and the ant palette no longer reaches for the terracotta key).
- ant-farm 0.2.3: a subagent that has made more requests than finished ones needed shows no time left (the bar stays nearly full) instead of a fixed few seconds; a finished line gives its numbers up too when the pane is too narrow for them, so no row is ever wider than the pane.
- ant-farm 0.2.2: every card row is sized in cells so nothing wraps at two cards across: the status word and what follows it share one line (`WORKING · Bash npm test · 4s`), the ETA sits with the cost, the sparkline narrows on a narrow card, the tools and edits counts show only on a wide card; a finished line cuts its task before its numbers; a card's digit is its mission number (1 to 9); a held subagent counts as waiting, not as running too; the session line sits above the subagents' total.
- ant-farm 0.2.1: anything shaped like a credential in a subagent's tool words or an error's first words is replaced with [REDACTED] before the pane keeps it, so the pane stays safe to share; the farm also proves it draws state kept by an earlier version.
