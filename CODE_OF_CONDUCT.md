# Code of Conduct

modmyclaude is a small project built in the open. We want it to be a good place to share ideas and build mods, for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most mistakes are honest ones.
- Critique the code, the mod or the idea, never the person.
- Give credit, and say when you are unsure.
- Help newcomers: everyone started with their first mod.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Mods, issues or pull requests that exist to harm users, such as hidden data collection or code that disables safety checks.
- Repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately: use **Report content** on the comment or issue and choose to report it to the repository's maintainers, or email the address on the [modmyclaude organisation](https://github.com/modmyclaude) profile. Reports are read by maintainers only and handled in confidence.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what is fair for the situation: a private note, a public warning, hiding or editing content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project. We will tell the person reported what the decision is and why.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
